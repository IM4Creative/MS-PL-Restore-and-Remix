# Examples from "What it can do"

Full-size before and after images. The after images were made in Gemini (Pro, Create image) on 2026-10-17 with these prompts.

| Practice | Before | After | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · Restore | [A-restore-before.jpg](A-restore-before.jpg) | [A-restore-after.png](A-restore-after.png) | Restore this photo. Remove the scratches, creases, dust and tears. Keep the face, hair and clothing exactly as shown. Keep the sepia tone. Keep everything else unchanged. |
| B · Targeted edits | [B-targeted-edit-before.jpg](B-targeted-edit-before.jpg) | [B-targeted-edit-after.png](B-targeted-edit-after.png) | Remove only the spoon on the saucer. Fill the gap with the saucer. Keep the lighting and shadows unchanged. Keep everything else unchanged. |
| C · Redesign | [C-redesign-before.png](C-redesign-before.png) | [C-redesign-after.png](C-redesign-after.png) | Preserve the room's walls, windows and camera angle. Redesign it in mid-century modern style using walnut, brass and wool. Keep the layout identical. |
| D · Style | [D-style-before.jpg](D-style-before.jpg) | [D-style-after.png](D-style-after.png) | Restyle this image as a Bauhaus poster with flat red, yellow, and black shapes. Keep likeness identical. Keep the markings and composition unchanged. |

The empty room in C was also made in Gemini. Before photos: Grace Hopper (US Navy, public domain; damage added digitally), coffee cup (Rachel Michetti, CC0), Chelsea the cat (Stefan van der Walt, CC0).
